Methodology

A repeatable testing mindset.

The objective is not a noisy scanner dump. It is a defensible story: where trust breaks, how the issue reproduces, why it matters, and what should change.

01

Recon & mapping

Map the attack surface, technologies, services, identities and trust boundaries.

02

Manual discovery

Understand roles, flows, assumptions and behavior through hands-on interaction.

03

Validation & exploitation

Use the smallest safe proof needed to establish exploitability and meaningful impact.

04

Impact thinking

Where scope permits, assess escalation, lateral movement, persistence and exposure.

05

Reporting & remediation

Document evidence, severity, reproduction, root cause and practical remediation.
